Tran code drill

Common tran codes

    Most codes have two parts. The first tells you the source of the money, the second where it is going. Cash: CR in, CD out. Checks: KR to deposit, KC to cash. Buying or selling your drawer or cash with another teller: CR GL and CD GL.
